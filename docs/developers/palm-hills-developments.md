# Palm Hills Developments

## Who They Are

Palm Hills Developments is an Egyptian developer established in 1997. The company says it focuses on integrated residential, commercial, and resort developments, with a portfolio spanning West Cairo, East Cairo, Alexandria, and the North Coast. Its corporate overview reports a land bank of approximately 40.5 million square metres and 50 projects; these are company-published portfolio figures and may change over time. [Company overview](https://www.palmhillsdevelopments.com/who-we-are/about-us)

## Main Projects

The company's official residential portfolio includes developments such as Badya, Bamboo, and Palm Hills New Cairo. The company identifies Palm Hills New Cairo as a 500-feddan integrated development in East Cairo. [Residential portfolio](https://www.palmhillsdevelopments.com/en-us/residential-properties) [Company overview](https://ir.palmhillsdevelopments.com/en-us/)

The team's collected listings include Cleo - Palm Hills New Cairo, with Palm Hills Developments named as the developer. [Cleo unit listing](https://www.nawy.com/compound/663-cleo-palm-hills-new-cairo/property/70405-apartment-for-sale-in-cleo-palm-hills-new-cairo-with-21-bedrooms-in-new-cairo-by-palm-hills-developments)

## Payment Plans

The collected Cleo listing shows one unit-specific plan: an initial payment of EGP 317,490, escalating monthly installments starting from EGP 7,442, and additional bulk payments. The listing also displays a separate maintenance amount. These figures describe the particular listing and should not be treated as a standard payment policy for every Palm Hills project or unit. Buyers should verify the current offer and full payment schedule before relying on it. [Cleo payment details](https://www.nawy.com/compound/663-cleo-palm-hills-new-cairo/property/70405-apartment-for-sale-in-cleo-palm-hills-new-cairo-with-21-bedrooms-in-new-cairo-by-palm-hills-developments)

## Delivery Track Record

Palm Hills' investor-relations website reports 20,000 delivered units among its key indicators. This is a company-reported aggregate figure; the page does not provide a project-by-project delivery schedule next to the indicator. [Investor-relations overview](https://ir.palmhillsdevelopments.com/en-us/)

The company also publishes project announcements. For example, its June 2025 announcement about Jirian describes the project scope, location, and planned development phases; it is a project announcement, not evidence that the project has been delivered. [Jirian announcement](https://ir.palmhillsdevelopments.com/en-us/newsevents/newsarticle/palm-hills-developments-unveils-palm-hills-jirian-a-transformative-residential-project-featuring-a-branch-of-the-nile-river-in-new-sheikh-zayed-spanning-361-feddan)
