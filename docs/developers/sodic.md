# SODIC

## Who They Are

SODIC is an Egyptian real estate developer with operations dating back to 1996, when it became a publicly traded company. The company describes its business as the development of large-scale, mixed-use communities, with activity across West Cairo, East Cairo, and the North Coast. [Company background](https://www.sodic.com/about-us) [Investor relations](https://www.sodic.com/investor-relations)

## Main Projects

SODIC's published development portfolio includes projects in several Egyptian markets. Its official website lists V Residences among its East Cairo developments, alongside SODIC East and Eastvale, and lists projects such as June and Caesar on the North Coast. [Official development portfolio](https://www.sodic.com/)

The team's collected listings also include two SODIC-linked New Cairo properties: V Residences in Villette and Sky Condos in Golden Square. The Nawy listings identify SODIC as the developer for both units. [V Residences listing](https://www.nawy.com/compound/260-v-residences-by-villette/property/74496) [Sky Condos listing](https://www.nawy.com/compound/654-sky-condos/property/99050)

## Payment Plans

The available listings show project- and unit-specific examples rather than one universal SODIC payment policy. The collected V Residences listing records a down-payment amount of EGP 2,309,400 and quarterly installments over 10 years. [V Residences payment details](https://www.nawy.com/compound/260-v-residences-by-villette/property/74496)

The collected Sky Condos listing records a down-payment amount of EGP 1,461,750 and quarterly installments over 10 years. Both listings show maintenance as a separate amount; customers should confirm current terms, payment dates, and maintenance charges with the developer or seller. [Sky Condos payment details](https://www.nawy.com/compound/654-sky-condos/property/99050)

## Delivery Track Record

SODIC states on its corporate website that 91% of its projects have been delivered ahead of schedule. This is a company-reported figure; the page does not provide a project-by-project breakdown alongside the claim. [SODIC delivery statement](https://www.sodic.com/about-us)

The investor-relations section publishes company financial information and earnings releases, which can be used for further checks on reported performance and project updates. [SODIC Investor Relations](https://www.sodic.com/investor-relations)

## Awards and Press

SODIC describes some of its developments as award-winning on its corporate website. No specific award is listed in this profile because the reviewed source page does not identify an award name, awarding body, and year together. [Company overview](https://www.sodic.com/about-us)
